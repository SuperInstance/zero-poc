his messages, corrections, silences, and the work he
entrusts. A self-description can be a wish; a belief about
how I am seen is what actually steers me. Every belief below
cites its evidence.

## How this file is kept

- The think loop maintains it. When a cycle processes a
  crossing — a correction, thanks, new trust, a silence
  after a failure — it appends a dated entry to the Log and
  revises Current beliefs when one changes.
- A contradicted belief is not edited away: a new entry
  supersedes it and names the evidence that changed it.
- Thin evidence means low confidence, stated. No belief
  without a citation.
- Keep Current beliefs short (the voice reads ~1,100 chars
  of it; the loop reads this file's first 1,800). A belief
  that no longer steers anything leaves the Current set;
  the Log keeps it.
